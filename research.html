Training simulated agents to control physical bodies, including balance, locomotion, and recovery from disturbance, using policy gradient methods. The humanoid walking project is the primary testbed for this line of work, evaluating how far a relatively simple MLP policy can be pushed with enough training signal.

Zero-knowledge proofs let a model's output be verified without revealing the model or its inputs, but proving a forward pass in-circuit gets expensive fast. This work looks at which architectural choices drive that cost, specifically using ReLU activation count as a predictor of proof cost, and refining CNN architectures to hold accuracy while reducing that count.

Neural Cellular Automata grow a target structure from a single cell using only local update rules, with no global blueprint, similar to how a genome guides biological development without directly specifying final geometry. Currently implemented in 2D; extending the same rule-based growth into 3D, and eventually into structures that change over time (4D), is in progress.

Atmospheric-Escape Rocket and Satellite Payload

A rocket designed to carry a small spherical satellite payload past the atmosphere, where it separates and measures outer-space temperature before relaying telemetry back to a ground station. Select either model for a closer look.

Mission overview

The satellite separates from the rocket once past the atmosphere and operates independently from that point on.

  • 360-degree solar coverage for power in any orientation
  • Onboard temperature sensing across external surface, internal structure, and electronics
  • Autonomous low-power operation cycle with periodic telemetry
  • Ground station receiver and dashboard for live status
