About

What AxA is, and who's behind it.

AxA is an independent robotics and machine learning research practice. It exists to demonstrate finished and in-progress work, from simulated robots and computer-vision systems to generative models, in one place, with the actual demo alongside the explanation rather than a link pointing elsewhere.

The name comes from the intersection at the center of the work: autonomy and algorithms, robotics and research. Each project here sits somewhere on that line, from a fully trained simulation to an early concept sketch.
